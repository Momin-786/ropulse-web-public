# Service section visual direction

## Current unified treatment

The owner requested the same aesthetic across all four panels. All now share
the plum background, supplied RP brand mark, serif lettering, magenta full stop,
eyebrow, ruled signoff and matching white caption area. Their content is driven
by one shared component template. Photography and mixed panel treatments are
no longer used in this section. Tablet and phone layouts use two columns, with
consistent card proportions and reduced-motion support.

## Previous iteration and retained asset provenance

The owner rejected the initial outline-icon row and requested logos or stronger
visuals. The replacement uses an editorial layout with four linked panels:

- Cafés: large serif lettering on the brand's lavender surface.
- Quick service: the existing app's burger photography and a short headline.
- Dine-in: the existing app's pizza photography and table-service lettering.
- Multiple branches: the supplied Ropulse RP monogram on plum, with schematic
  branch labels. The labels do not identify real customer locations.

The retail handoff uses the supplied Ropulse wordmark, a clear heading and a
direct link. No customer logos, endorsements or fictional restaurant brands
were added. Sector cards link to the relevant restaurant or platform page.
Subtle photo zoom and card movement are disabled with reduced motion.

The two JPGs in `public/images/business/` are unchanged copies of the provided
app snapshot's `packages/ropulse_backoffice/assets/demo_products/burger.jpg`
and `pizza.jpg`. They already appear in its sample catalog. They are contextual
menu photography, not photographs of Ropulse customer premises. No external
stock photos were downloaded; the attempted download was unavailable.
