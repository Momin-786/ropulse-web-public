## Privacy notice

- Public business/postal address and the privacy contact, if different from support.
- The actual enquiry receiver/storage provider and who can access enquiries.
- Retention period for enquiries and operational logs; deletion request procedure.
- Hosting/logging locations, processors and any transfers the operator must describe.
- Whether analytics, marketing emails or tracking will be enabled. None are
  implemented in the current website; consent to an enquiry does not subscribe
  someone to marketing.

The current form cannot submit until the approved notice and receiving service
are configured. It collects name, business name, email or phone, country, branch
count and explicit permission to respond. The app does not log enquiry bodies
or store enquiries in a website database. The hosting provider and eventual
receiver need their own reviewed logging/retention settings.
