IDENTITY / 01

Brand guidelines & restaurant product design direction

A modern restaurant ordering pulse.

Design specification and visual concepts. The generated logo reference is raster artwork; production vector masters and implemented

## Page 2

FOUNDATION / 02
The restaurant runs in rhythm.

Positioning

ROPULSE is a restaurant SaaS platform connecting how guests order, how teams fulfil, how ingredients move
and how owners understand performance. It should feel equally credible for a neighbourhood café and a
multi-branch restaurant group.

Brand promise

Give every team a clear view of what needs to happen next, from the first tap to the final report.

Connected

The same order remains recognisable at the counter, in the kitchen and in reporting.

Clear

Make totals, modifiers, statuses and next steps easy to understand during a rush.

In control

Surface exceptions with context: late tickets, low stock, waste and performance changes.

Adaptable

Support quick service, dine-in, cafés, cloud kitchens and multi-branch operations through configurable workflows.

Personality

Confident, practical, welcoming and precise. Use rhythm and flow as visual ideas; communicate business value
with concrete language.

## Page 4

LOGO / 04
Protect the flow.

Primary identity

Use the chosen geometric ROPULSE wordmark, preserving the distinctive R and flowing magenta U/L
treatment. The RP monogram is the compact app identity. Never typeset a substitute and call it the master
logo.

Approved lockups

1. Horizontal wordmark for website navigation, sales material and invoices.
2. Wordmark plus tagline for introductions and brand communications.
3. RP monogram for app tiles, favicons and compact navigation.
4. One-colour plum or black for receipts; reversed white on plum for dark backgrounds.

Clear space

Define x as the height of the wordmark letters. Keep at least 0.25x empty around the wordmark and 0.20 of
icon width around the RP mark. Measure from the visible artwork, not its canvas. Keep the tagline inside a
composed lockup, with 0.20x vertical separation.

Minimum sizes

Wordmark: 140 px digital / 30 mm print. Tagline lockup: 280 px / 60 mm. RP symbol: 24 px; at 16 px use a
hand-tuned simplified favicon. App store export: 1024 × 1024 px before platform masking. Verify legibility on
actual thermal printers.

Never

Stretch, rotate, recolour arbitrarily, add shadows, outline the mark, change the U/L shape, use low-contrast
placement or put it over busy food photography. Do not place the concept number “05” in any production use.

Production step: redraw the selected mark accurately as vector paths, approve letter recognition at small sizes, then export SVG, PDF and
transparent PNG variants. The supplied concept image is the visual reference.

Use neutral surfaces for roughly 75–85% of working screens. Reserve magenta for the strongest action and selected state. Dark

## Page 6

ACCESSIBILITY / 06
Clarity is part of the identity.

Approved reading pairs

Ink or plum on warm white; white on plum; white on Pulse Magenta for primary controls. The build handoff
includes calculated contrast ratios in the token appendix. Do not use lavender for text or rely on a gradient
behind small labels.

Semantic colour system

Success #137A52 / pale #E9F7EF: paid, completed, synced.
Warning #8A4B00 / pale #FFF3D6: approaching time limit, low stock.
Danger #B42318 / pale #FDECE9: failed payment, critical delay, destructive action.
Info #1D4ED8 / pale #EAF0FF: informational updates.
Every state also has an icon and plain-language label. Thresholds belong to operational settings.

Interaction requirements

Target WCAG 2.2 AA in implementation: 4.5:1 for ordinary text, 3:1 for large text and meaningful UI
boundaries. Use a visible 2 px focus ring with an offset. Keep controls at least 44 × 44 px for staff touch
screens, preferably 48–56 px on kiosks.

More than colour

Show “Late · 08:42” rather than a red ticket alone. Pair chart colours with direct labels or patterns. Preserve
keyboard order, screen-reader names, zoom and reduced-motion settings. Never flash alerts.

Dark mode

Use plum as a shell, with contrasting raised surfaces and readable labels. Maintain the same semantic status
meanings. Create and test a separate dark token set before calling it supported; the present application
concepts prioritise light workspaces.

## Page 7

TYPOGRAPHY / 07
Distinctive headings. Easy reading.

Recommended type families

Manrope Semibold/Bold for brand and marketing headings. Inter Regular/Medium/Semibold for application UI
and body text. Use tabular numerals for money, quantities and timers. The logo lettering remains custom
artwork. This PDF uses DejaVu Sans as a document fallback.

Marketing scale

Hero 56/64 px desktop, 36/42 mobile. H1 40/48; H2 32/40; H3 24/32. Body 18/28 for landing pages. Keep reading lines
around 60–75 characters.

Application scale

Page heading 28/36. Section heading 20/28. Body and inputs 16/24. Dense tables 14/20. Labels 13/18. Never reduce
critical ticket modifiers or totals below 16 px on working touch displays.

Weights & casing

Use 400, 500, 600 and 700. Sentence case for UI; uppercase only for short navigation eyebrows or the wordmark.
Avoid wide letter spacing on long text.

Localisation

Externalise all strings. Allow 30–40% text expansion. For Arabic use a compatible Arabic family such as Noto Sans
Arabic; for Urdu use a tested readable Urdu family. Support RTL layout without mirroring the logo, product photos or
numeric values.

Financial formatting

Show currency unambiguously: PKR 2,450.00. Label tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive amounts. Dates, decimal places,
currency and timezone follow the merchant configuration; label dashboard timezone and range.

## Page 8

VISUAL LANGUAGE / 08
Use the pulse with restraint.

Graphic motif

Use one broad flowing curve inspired by the U/L stroke on covers, hero sections and dividers. Crop it
confidently at the canvas edge. Keep it away from tables, operational alerts and long text. A plum-to-magenta
gradient is an optional marketing accent; use solid colours for essential UI.

Photography

Show authentic restaurant service: hands using a POS, kitchen handover, cafés, food preparation and owners
checking results. Use natural light, warm food tones and real-looking environments. Avoid staged handshakes
and generic futuristic imagery. Show branded UI where permission permits.

Iconography

Use one consistent rounded line icon family on a 24 px grid, with about 2 px strokes. Recommended symbols:
terminal for POS, ticket for Kitchen, bag for Order, touch screen for Kiosk, QR for QR, box for Inventory, chart
for Insights. Pair module icons with labels.

Shape & motion

Cards: 12–16 px radius. Inputs: 8 px. Primary buttons: 10 px. App tiles may use 20–24% corner rounding
before platform masks. UI transitions: 120–200 ms. Use a single quiet pulse on a successful state change,
never continuous motion in the KDS.

Co-branding

Guest-facing ordering is led by the restaurant’s name, menu photography and approved merchant palette.
Keep “Powered by ROPULSE” discreet in the footer or help area. Operational applications retain the ROPULSE
shell.

Supporting message

Connect your counter, kitchen, digital orders and stock in one clear workspace.

Calls to action

Book a demo · Explore the platform · See Kitchen · Start an order. Use one primary action per decision area.

Success message

Order #1084 sent to Kitchen. Table 08 · Dine-in.

Recoverable error

Payment wasn’t completed. Check the terminal before trying again.

Connection state

Connection lost. Last synced at 14:32. Availability of offline actions depends on the implemented capability; never
promise offline payment or fiscal submission without verified support.

Avoid “guaranteed profit”, “zero errors”, “fully compliant” or “real-time” unless evidence and system behaviour support the
claim. Describe what a feature does. Testimonials, integrations, prices and performance statistics need verified source material
before publication.

## Page 10

UI SYSTEM / 10
A shared framework for every module.

Spacing & layout

Use a 4 px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Desktop content: maximum 1280 px; marketing grid: 12 columns with 24
px gutters. App sidebar: 232–256 px; header: 64 px. Breakpoints: 480, 768, 1024 and 1280 px.

Buttons & inputs

Primary: magenta with white label. Hover: #B80059. Disabled: neutral with explicit disabled behaviour. Secondary:
white, plum text and visible border. Destructive: danger. Every field needs a persistent label, helpful error and
keyboard focus.

Surfaces

Warm-white page, white cards, #D8CEDD neutral borders. Avoid heavy shadows in dense apps. Use elevation only for
menus, dialogs and floating order baskets.

Tables

Right-align quantities and money. Put units in headers. Keep key identifiers visible when horizontally scrolling. Use
filters, sorting, pagination, export permissions and a clear zero-result state.

Charts

Use magenta as the focal series, with plum, blue and teal supporting series. Label legends and units. Compare
equivalent periods. Show empty, partial and stale data states; never connect missing points without explanation.

Feedback

Specify default, hover, active, focus, loading, disabled, empty, error and success states. Prevent repeated submission
while an action is processing. Confirm consequential voids/refunds and preserve an audit trail.

## Page 11

OPERATIONAL DESIGN / 11
Fast at the counter. Clear in the kitchen.

POS

Keep category navigation, menu, basket and payment visible. Support modifiers, mandatory choices, sold-out
items, table context, staff permissions and order notes. Make subtotal, tax, service charge, discount, rounding
and grand total separately readable. Warn about unsent edits before leaving an order.

Kitchen display

Use clear New → Preparing → Ready states with explicit station routing. Show elapsed time, order number,
fulfilment type, table and modifiers on every relevant ticket. Differentiate allergies from ordinary notes with
text and a symbol. Require acknowledgement for critical actions; allow controlled recall of a completed ticket.

Resilience

Always show connection health and last successful sync. If offline operation is built, show queued actions,
device ownership and sync conflicts; distinguish cached data from confirmed server actions. Recovery should
preserve order identifiers and avoid duplicate payment or kitchen submission.

Receipt & print

Provide a monochrome wordmark for 58/80 mm thermal receipts and a clean A4 invoice layout. Include
merchant identity, order number, line items, taxes and payment summary according to configuration. Fiscal
fields and QR codes depend on validated jurisdiction requirements, never decorative brand marks.

Role focus

Cashiers see ordering and permitted adjustments. Kitchen staff see fulfilment. Managers see shifts, exceptions
and stock actions. Owners see cross-branch outcomes. UI visibility reinforces permissions; server-side
authorisation must enforce them.

## Page 12

GUEST EXPERIENCES / 12
The restaurant leads. Ordering feels
effortless.

Online ordering

Start with location and fulfilment: pickup or delivery where offered. Show availability and operating hours
before checkout. Make modifiers, allergens, delivery fees, minimum order and estimated timing clear. Permit
guest checkout when business rules allow; loyalty enrolment remains optional.

QR at the table

Open directly to a merchant-branded menu with a visible restaurant and table identifier. Allow the guest to
verify table context. Distinguish “Send to kitchen”, “Pay now” and “Request bill” according to configuration.
Show a confirmed order summary and recovery path if the network drops.

Self-service kiosk

Use large targets, food images, short labels, visible basket and a clear way back. Display running total
throughout. Separate dine-in/takeaway selection from payment. Provide an accessible reach zone, timeout
warning, language switch and staff help. Remove previous guest information when a session ends.

Trust & consent

Separate marketing consent from service notifications. Avoid preselected loyalty consent. Explain cancelled,
failed and pending payments clearly. Never show full payment details. Give guests an order reference and a
contact route when fulfilment needs help.

Brand boundary

Use merchant styling on the storefront, while maintaining readable controls, accessible contrast and
consistent ordering behaviour. Keep the ROPULSE signature secondary to the restaurant.

## Page 13

STOCK & INSIGHT / 13
Measure what the team can act on.

Inventory & recipes

Always show quantity with units and location. Distinguish base units, purchase packs and recipe units. Recipes
need yield, portions, ingredient quantities, conversion factors, waste assumptions, cost version and effective
date. Use approved substitutions and modifiers in consumption rules.

Stock events

Purchasing, receiving, transfers, adjustments, counts and waste should be separate events with reason, staff
member and timestamp. Use approval rules for sensitive adjustments. Flag negative stock; do not silently
repair it. Explain whether availability is cached, counted or calculated.

Owner dashboard

Show sales, orders, average order value, channel mix, branch comparison, ticket time and stock exceptions. A
small set of useful KPIs is better than a wall of charts. Make each card actionable by linking to its detailed
report.

Metric definitions

Net sales: item sales less discounts and refunds, excluding taxes; define service charges separately. AOV:
qualifying sales divided by qualifying paid orders in the same period. Theoretical food cost: configured recipe
cost for sold items / corresponding food sales. Actual usage: opening + receipts + inbound transfers −
outbound transfers − closing, adjusted for documented events. Explain different cost bases.

Reporting context

Display period, timezone, currency, branch, tax basis, refresh time and permissions. Compare equivalent
periods. Distinguish theoretical from actual food cost and freshness from completeness. All figures in the
visual concepts are illustrative demo data.

## Page 14

WEBSITE / 14
Tell one connected story.

Navigation

Platform: POS, Kitchen, Order, Kiosk, QR, Inventory, Insights. Solutions: cafés, QSR, dine-in and multi-branch.
Resources, Sign in and Book a demo.

Homepage sequence

Hero and two actions → connected module overview → order-to-kitchen story → stock and recipe control → owner
insights → demo steps → final CTA → footer.

Module pages

Lead with the operational problem, show the relevant screen, explain 3–5 key workflows, state dependencies and end
with a focused demo invitation.

Demo conversion

Request name, business email/phone, restaurant name, country and branch count. Explain follow-up and link privacy
information. Keep the form short. Show a confirmation state and contact alternative.

Responsive behaviour

At tablet sizes simplify the top navigation and retain the demo CTA. At mobile sizes stack content, keep 16 px minimum
body text and remove decorative hero overlaps. Dashboard cards stack; tables use labelled horizontal scroll or a
purpose-built list.

Publication rules

Use real product screens when implemented. Label concept previews. Publish pricing, supported integrations, fiscal
support and availability only after commercial and technical verification. Footer should include contact, privacy, terms
and company identity.

## Page 15

APPLICATIONS / 15
Carry the system beyond the screen.

Sales deck & one-page brochure

Plum cover, wordmark, one pulse curve and a single benefit-led headline. Use real workflows, concise module cards
and one demo CTA. Keep dates and version labels visible.

Social media

Build 1080 × 1080 and 1080 × 1350 templates with a 64 px safe inset. One headline, one product crop and one CTA.
Keep the RP icon small and consistent. Do not squeeze every module into one post.

Business card & email

White front with wordmark and plum text; plum reverse with RP icon. Email signature includes name, role, contact and
verified website. Avoid embedding the tagline as unreadable small image text.

Operational print

Monochrome receipts, readable table QR stands and clear kiosk instruction signs. Verify every live QR destination
before printing; concept QR patterns are placeholders only.

Partner use

Maintain separation between partner and ROPULSE marks, with equal perceived size and independent clear space. Use
an accurate relationship label such as integration partner only when approved.

Asset naming

ROPULSE_Wordmark_Primary_RGB.svg; ROPULSE_Wordmark_White.svg; ROPULSE_RP_App_1024.png;
ROPULSE_Wordmark_Mono_Print.pdf. Keep approved assets in one versioned source folder.
